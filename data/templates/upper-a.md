## Bench Press

4 × 5–6
Rest: 2–3 min
Target: RPE 7–8

Feet planted. Control the touch, press evenly.

Equipment: Barbell, Bench

## Bent-over Row

3 × 8–10
Rest: 2 min
Target: RPE 7–8

Hinge and brace. Pull to the lower ribs.

Equipment: Barbell

## Overhead Press

3 × 8–10
Rest: 2 min
Target: RPE 7

Glutes tight. Head through at the top.

Equipment: Barbell

## Chest-supported Row

3 × 10–12
Rest: 90 sec

Pause one second at the squeeze.

Equipment: Dumbbell, Bench

## Push-up

2 × 8–12
Rest: 75 sec

Body in a line. Full lockout.

Equipment: Bodyweight

## Face Pull

2 × 12–15
Rest: 60 sec

Lead with the elbows. Light and crisp.

Equipment: Cable
