## Warm-up

Easy jog 3–5 min until breathing settles.

## Main

30–50 min easy. Conversational. Nasal breathing is a useful governor.

Intensity: RPE 3–4 / 10

## Recovery

None beyond staying easy. If you surge, slow down.

## Cooldown

Walk 2–3 min.

## Progression

Add 5–10 min when the run feels genuinely easy, not when the calendar says so.
