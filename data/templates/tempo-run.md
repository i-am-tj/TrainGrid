type: running
description: Comfortably hard, sustainable effort.
---

## Warm-up

10 min easy jog
2 × 20s strides

## Main

20 min continuous tempo, or 3 × 8 min with 2 min jog
Even breathing. You can speak a short phrase, not a sentence.

Intensity: RPE 6–7 / 10

## Recovery

Easy jog between tempo blocks if you split them.

## Cooldown

8–10 min easy jog

## Progression

20 min → 25 min, or add a third block before you chase pace.
