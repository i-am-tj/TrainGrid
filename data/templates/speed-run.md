type: running

## Warm-up

1 km easy jog
Dynamic drills: leg swings, A-skips, high knees
4 × 20s relaxed strides with walk-back

## Main

6–8 × 150–200m fast
Stay tall and snappy. Not a flattening sprint.

Intensity: RPE 8–9 / 10, full recovery between reps

## Recovery

Walk or very easy jog until you could go again cleanly (often 2–3 min).

## Cooldown

1 km easy jog

## Progression

Keep the distance short. Add 1–2 reps before you add speed.
