name: Lower B
type: strength
description: Hinge-pattern lower body.
---

## Deadlift

3 × 4–6
Rest: 3 min
Target: RPE 7–8

Neutral spine. Push the floor away.

Equipment: Barbell

## Front Squat

3 × 6–8
Rest: 2–3 min
Target: RPE 7

Elbows high. Sit between the hips.

Equipment: Barbell

## Hip Thrust

3 × 8–12
Rest: 90 sec
Target: RPE 7–8

Pause at lockout. Ribs down.

Equipment: Barbell, Bench

## Bulgarian Split Squat

3 × 8 / side
Rest: 90 sec

Slight forward lean is fine. Keep the front heel down.

Equipment: Dumbbell, Bench

## Hamstring Curl

3 × 10–12
Rest: 75 sec

Control the eccentric.

Equipment: Machine

## Back Extension

2 × 10–12
Rest: 60 sec

Squeeze glutes at the top. Do not hyperextend the neck.

Equipment: Bodyweight
