## Back Squat

4 × 5–6
Rest: 2–3 min
Target: RPE 7–8

Controlled descent. Drive aggressively out of the bottom.

Equipment: Barbell

## Pause Squat

3 × 4
Rest: 2 min
Target: RPE 7

2-second pause in the hole. Stay braced.

Equipment: Barbell

## Romanian Deadlift

3 × 8–10
Rest: 2 min
Target: RPE 7

Keep the bar close. Soft knees, long hamstrings.

Equipment: Barbell

## Bulgarian Split Squat

3 × 8 / side
Rest: 90 sec
Target: RPE 7–8

Front foot planted. Control the descent.

Equipment: Dumbbell, Bench

## Walking Lunge

2 × 10 / side
Rest: 90 sec

Even steps. Torso tall.

Equipment: Dumbbell

## Calf Raise

3 × 10–15
Rest: 60 sec

Full range. Pause at the top.

Equipment: Dumbbell
