## Easy Jog

5–10 min

## Leg Swings

10 / side

Front-to-back and side-to-side.

Equipment: Bodyweight

## Walking Lunges

10 / side

Long posture. Gentle stretch, not a workout.

Equipment: Bodyweight

## A-Skips

2 × 20m

Equipment: Bodyweight

## High Knees

2 × 20m

Equipment: Bodyweight

## Strides

3 × 60m

Relax the arms. Walk back.
