## A-Skips

3 × 20m
Rest: walk back

Rhythm first. Dorsiflex and bounce.

Equipment: Bodyweight

## Bounds

3 × 20m
Rest: walk back

Cover ground. Soft, springy contacts.

Equipment: Bodyweight

## Single-Leg Pogos

3 × 10 / side
Rest: 60 sec

Small amplitude. Stay tall.

Equipment: Bodyweight

## Lateral Bounds

3 × 6 / side
Rest: 90 sec

Stick each landing before the next bound.

Equipment: Bodyweight

## Acceleration Strides

4 × 60m
Rest: walk back

Build for 40m, float 20m. Not a max sprint.

Equipment: Bodyweight
