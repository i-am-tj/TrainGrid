## Bodyweight Squat

2 × 10

Equipment: Bodyweight

## Reverse Lunge

2 × 8 / side

Equipment: Bodyweight

## Glute Bridge

2 × 12

Pause at the top.

Equipment: Bodyweight

## Leg Swings

10 / side

Equipment: Bodyweight

## Ankle Rocks

10 / side

Knee over toes, heel down.

Equipment: Bodyweight

## Ramp-up sets

2–4 light sets of the first compound lift. Add load, keep reps crisp.
