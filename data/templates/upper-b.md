## Overhead Press

4 × 5–6
Rest: 2–3 min
Target: RPE 7–8

Brace hard. Bar path close to the face.

Equipment: Barbell

## Pull-up

3 × 6–10
Rest: 2 min
Target: RIR 2–3

Full hang to chest toward the bar. Add load only if you own every rep.

Equipment: Pull-up Bar

## Incline Dumbbell Press

3 × 8–10
Rest: 2 min
Target: RPE 7–8

30–45° bench. Lower with control.

Equipment: Dumbbell, Bench

## One-arm Dumbbell Row

3 × 8–10 / side
Rest: 90 sec

Support the bench. Pull the elbow past the hip.

Equipment: Dumbbell, Bench

## Dip

2 × 6–10
Rest: 90 sec

Shoulders down. Stop before the bottom gets sloppy.

Equipment: Bodyweight

## Lateral Raise

2 × 12–15
Rest: 60 sec

Soft elbows. Stop at shoulder height.

Equipment: Dumbbell
