---

## Arm Circles

10 each way

Slow. Stay in a pain-free range.

Equipment: Bodyweight

## Scapular Push-up

2 × 8

Lock the elbows. Move only the shoulder blades.

Equipment: Bodyweight

## Band Pull-apart

2 × 12

Thumbs up. Stop before the ribs flare.

Equipment: Resistance Band

## Y-T-W Raise

1 × 8 each

Light. Face down on a bench if you have one.

Equipment: Bodyweight, Bench

## Ramp-up sets

2–4 light sets of the first press or pull.
