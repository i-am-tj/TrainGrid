## Warm-up

1 km easy jog
Dynamic drills
2–3 relaxed strides

## Main

6 × 200m hard
Even effort. Leave a little for the last two.

Intensity: RPE 8 / 10

## Recovery

200m walk/jog between repetitions

## Cooldown

1 km easy jog

## Progression

200m → 300m → 400m → 600m over future sessions, same recovery idea.
