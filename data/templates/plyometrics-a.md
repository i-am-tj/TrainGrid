## Pogo Jumps

3 × 20
Rest: 45–60 sec

Quick ground contact. Think stiff ankles.

Equipment: Bodyweight

## Countermovement Jumps

3 × 5
Rest: 90 sec

Dip and go. Soft, quiet landings.

Equipment: Bodyweight

## Box Jumps

4 × 4
Rest: 2 min

Step down. Do not rebound off the box.

Equipment: Plyometric Box

## Split-Squat Jumps

3 × 5 / side
Rest: 90 sec

Upright torso. Switch or stay on one side, then swap.

Equipment: Bodyweight

## Broad Jumps

3 × 4
Rest: 2 min

Stick the landing. Reset between reps.

Equipment: Bodyweight
