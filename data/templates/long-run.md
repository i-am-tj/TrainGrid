---

## Warm-up

First 10 min very easy. Shuffle if needed.

## Main

60–90 min easy. Optional last 10 min slightly quicker if you still feel smooth.

Intensity: RPE 3–5 / 10

## Recovery

Walk water stops as needed. Do not turn this into a workout.

## Cooldown

Walk 5 min.

## Progression

Add 10 min every 1–2 weeks, then take a shorter long run.
