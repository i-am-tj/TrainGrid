name: Recovery Run
type: running

---

## Warm-up

2–3 min walk, then ease into a jog.

## Main

20–30 min very easy. Slower than Easy Run is correct.

Intensity: RPE 2–3 / 10

## Recovery

If it stops feeling easy, walk.

## Cooldown

Walk 2–3 min. Easy breathing.

## Progression

Keep this short. Fitness comes from the other days.
